# PindahTangan — Partner & Operations Pilot Conversation Deck

**Audience:** prospective studio, intake/drop-off, pickup, host, and courier partners.

**Single-minded message:** PindahTangan is testing a small, managed fashion-consignment workflow; a partner should only commit to the discrete, documented part they can actually deliver.

**Decision status:** conversation material for a controlled pilot. It is not a launch announcement, an operating commitment, or a promise of transaction volume.

**Source basis:** `PindahTangan — Pilot Readiness Risk Register.md` (R-05, R-06, R-08, R-10, R-12, R-13, R-17); `docs/PILOT_BUSINESS_MODEL_AND_ECONOMICS.md` §§2, 4, 6–8; `docs/PINDAHTANGAN_BRAND_IDENTITY_AND_MESSAGING.md` §§1, 3–5. All participant counts, item counts, prices, and timing below are proposed test inputs unless an agreement is signed.

---

## Slide 1 — PindahTangan

**Konsinyasi fesyen terkelola — pilot conversation**

Pakaian yang masih bagus nggak harus diam di lemari. PindahTangan sedang merancang proses titip jual yang membantu pemilik pakaian melewati pengecekan, kurasi, presentasi, penjualan, dan pembaruan status dengan lebih jelas.

**What we are asking today:** explore a limited pilot role, not confirm a public launch.

---

## Slide 2 — The task we are trying to make lighter

For a wardrobe owner, resale often becomes a task that keeps getting postponed: photos, measurements, listing, negotiation, packing, and shipping.

PindahTangan’s proposed role is to coordinate those steps as a small managed-consignment workflow.

**Not a claim today:** established pickup coverage, studio availability, payout schedule, hygiene result, buyer volume, or live-sales result.

---

## Slide 3 — Proposed pilot flow

1. **Interest & intake request** — an interested consignor is contacted manually; any pickup/drop-off option is confirmed case by case.
2. **Receipt & QC** — every item is counted, assigned a temporary custody record, checked, and marked accepted or not accepted with a reason.
3. **Agreement** — accepted items receive a proposed **agreed net payout** before they are offered for sale.
4. **Merchandising & live** — approved items are prepared for a controlled sales session only after the partner/operator confirms readiness.
5. **Order & fulfillment** — only buyer-paid orders with complete, verified delivery details move to dispatch.
6. **Payout & reconciliation** — eligible consignor liabilities are prepared, approved, transferred, and reconciled with bank proof before any status is called complete.

**Important:** the current product deployment is prototype-only. This deck does not rely on its booking, payout, WhatsApp, order, or staff-route functions.

---

## Slide 4 — What a small pilot can look like

**Proposed learning cohort:** up to 5 consented consignors and up to 100 submitted items, subject to capacity and written rules.

**Proposed readiness gate for one session:** 50 saleable, priced, traceable items available before a session. This is a planning threshold, not a promise of demand.

**What we measure:** custody accuracy, QC acceptance/rejection reasons, time and cost per item, paid-item conversion, payment-to-dispatch rate, payout accuracy, participant feedback, and exceptions.

**Stop conditions:** any unresolved custody, buyer-payment, shipping, or payout-reconciliation exception; any partner role performed without the agreed record; or a material safety/privacy issue.

---

## Slide 5 — Studio / intake partner conversation

**Role being explored**
- Receive items only during mutually agreed windows.
- Provide a practical space for counting, checking, hanging, and documenting accepted items.
- Preserve a chain of custody: who handed over, when, how many, and where items are stored.

**Questions to resolve before a pilot**
- Is the space available, appropriate, and permitted for this use?
- What access, storage, handling, and damage/loss rules apply?
- Who is the named on-site operator and what are the escalation contacts?
- What cost, schedule, and cancellation terms apply?

**Do not imply:** permanent studio address, guaranteed operating hours, or professional hygiene treatment until documented and verified.

---

## Slide 6 — Pickup / courier partner conversation

**Role being explored**
- A manually confirmed handover or transport leg for a defined pilot batch.
- Receipt at handover showing count, condition caveat, handler, date/time, and destination.
- A clear exception path for a missed handover, mismatch, damage concern, or delay.

**Questions to resolve**
- Coverage, pickup windows, capacity, pricing, service terms, and insurance/liability.
- Whether the provider can issue a verifiable receipt or tracking reference.
- How an address and contact detail are handled and retained.

**Boundary:** no “free pickup,” service-area, or courier-contact promise is communicated to consignors until partner terms and capacity are confirmed.

---

## Slide 7 — Host / live partner conversation

**Role being explored**
- Present only a prepared, priced, traceable item queue.
- Confirm the live platform, date, content rights, conduct, compensation, and cancellation terms in writing.
- Record claimed buyer, item, agreed price, payment confirmation, and exceptions through the pilot ledger.

**Learning question:** can a curated, accurately described selection produce buyer-paid orders without compromising condition clarity or process discipline?

**Boundary:** no audience size, sales result, commission, or platform integration is assumed. A “mark sold” prototype click is not evidence of a buyer-paid order.

---

## Slide 8 — Courier / fulfillment partner conversation

**Role being explored**
- Dispatch only after the buyer’s payment and full delivery details are verified.
- Use provider-issued tracking or a manually verified reference; never generated placeholder tracking.
- Return dispatch status and exception evidence to the order ledger.

**Questions to resolve**
- Service area, price basis, liability for loss/damage/failed delivery, claims process, and proof-of-dispatch/delivery.

**Boundary:** shipping is a buyer pass-through in the proposed model and is reconciled separately from item-sale proceeds.

---

## Slide 9 — Controls that protect everyone

- One custody record per submitted item/batch; no untraceable item proceeds.
- QC decision and reason; no implied acceptance.
- Agreed net payout documented before sale; no hidden post-sale deduction.
- Buyer payment, seller liability, shipping, refund/return, and payout are separate ledger states.
- Payout is complete only after a verified bank reference and reconciliation.
- No personal data is placed in the current prototype or casually shared in chat.

**Current gate:** no real-money activity or public operational launch while the audited product security/persistence controls remain unresolved.

---

## Slide 10 — What we need from a pilot partner

1. A named person who can decide on their operational slice.
2. Written scope: role, location/coverage, capacity, cost, cancellation, privacy, and incident path.
3. A short dry-run before participant items or customer money are involved.
4. Permission to pause if an agreed control fails.

**Next step:** a 30-minute scope-and-risk conversation, followed by a one-page written pilot role sheet. No partner is treated as confirmed until that sheet is agreed.
