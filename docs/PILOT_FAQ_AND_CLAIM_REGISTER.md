# PindahTangan — Pilot FAQ & Claim Register

**Audience:** team members answering consignor, partner, and pilot questions.

**Single-minded message:** answer clearly about what is being tested; never turn an intended workflow or prototype screen into a fact.

**Source basis:** `PindahTangan — Pilot Readiness Risk Register.md`; `PindahTangan — Product & UX Audit.md`; `docs/PILOT_BUSINESS_MODEL_AND_ECONOMICS.md`; `docs/PINDAHTANGAN_BRAND_IDENTITY_AND_MESSAGING.md`. The audit verdict is controlling for present capability.

## Approved FAQ wording

### What is PindahTangan?
PindahTangan is a proposed managed fashion-consignment service. In the pilot, we are testing a small, documented process for receiving usable clothes, checking them, preparing them for sale, and giving the owner a clearer view of what happens next.

### Is PindahTangan already open to everyone?
Not yet. The current work is a limited pilot conversation and controlled operational test. Capacity, participant eligibility, and the next step are confirmed individually.

### Can I request pickup?
You can ask about an option to hand over items. Any pickup, drop-off, area, timing, cost, and capacity must be confirmed before it is promised. We do not state a pickup guarantee in the pilot invitation.

### What kind of clothes can I submit?
The team will explain the current pilot criteria and assess items individually. An item is not accepted until it has been received, checked, and recorded.

### How is my payout decided?
For an accepted item, the pilot model uses an **agreed net payout** that is discussed and recorded before the item is offered for sale. It is not an earnings estimate and does not guarantee that the item will sell.

### When will I receive payment?
A payout is eligible only after a buyer payment is verified and any agreed fulfillment, cancellation, refund, or return hold has passed. The exact method and timing must be explained in the pilot terms; do not promise automatic or Friday payment at this stage.

### Are items cleaned or sterilised?
Do not make a hygiene, sterilisation, steam-temperature, odour, or safety guarantee. State only the actual, documented handling process after it has been verified.

### Can I track the process?
The team aims to give participants clear updates during the pilot. The specific update channel and timing will be confirmed with the participant; the current prototype portal is not a production record.

### What happens if an item is not accepted, does not sell, is returned, or is damaged?
Those outcomes must be covered by written pilot terms before any real item enters the process. Do not improvise a donation, buyout, return, compensation, or retention promise.

### Is the website ready to use for booking or payout?
No. The audited deployment is a local-state prototype and must not be used to collect real bookings, bank details, payments, or operational commitments. Use the approved human-managed pilot contact route instead.

## Quick response rules

1. If the answer depends on location, capacity, price, partner, timing, payment, hygiene, or a participant’s item status, check the actual record or say it will be confirmed.
2. Never use a prototype UI state, seed record, generated receipt, default customer detail, or simulated WhatsApp message as proof.
3. Never collect bank details, passwords, or real customer addresses through the audited prototype.
4. Escalate a question about legal terms, consumer rights, tax, payment custody, loss/damage, refund/return, or data handling to the named owner before answering definitively.
5. When in doubt, use: “Yang bisa kami pastikan saat ini adalah [verified fact]. Untuk [uncertain point], kami cek dulu dan kembali dengan detailnya.”
